## Full description (4,000 characters max)
PassKey is an offline password manager that keeps your logins, PINs and notes in an encrypted vault on your phone.
It has no account, no cloud and no ads. Your vault never leaves your phone.

WHY PASSKEY
• Offline by design: the vault is an encrypted database on this device, and nothing you save is ever sent anywhere.
• Its own key: your vault is encrypted with a key created on your phone and kept in Android's secure hardware,
  unlocked with your fingerprint or screen lock.
• A recovery password you choose, so a change of fingerprints or screen lock never locks you out.

FILL LOGINS IN SECONDS
• Autofill in your apps and in Chrome. PassKey always asks for your fingerprint or PIN before filling, and shows no
  entry names until you do.
• Careful matching: look-alike apps and websites are not offered your logins.
• Save new logins straight from the sign-in screen.

TWO-FACTOR CODES
• Add authenticator (TOTP) codes from the setup link or the key a website shows you.
• Tap a code to copy it. Copied codes and passwords are hidden from the clipboard preview and cleared automatically.

STRONG PASSWORDS
• A password generator with the length and character types you choose.
• A safety check for weak and reused passwords.
• Password history keeps the last 10 versions of every password.

BACKUPS YOU CONTROL
• An encrypted backup file (.pkbak), protected by a password you choose, that you can restore on any phone.
• It also imports the older PassKey CSV backups.

PRIVATE AND SAFE BY DEFAULT
• Auto-lock after a time you choose, and whenever Android restores the app from memory.
• Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked.
• Delete with Undo.
• Crash reports and usage statistics are off until you choose to share them, and never include anything you save.

FOR EVERYONE
• 17 languages: English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, தமிழ், తెలుగు, عربي, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano,
  Português, Русский, 日本語, 한국어, 中文.
• Dark mode, large-text support and screen-reader labels.
• Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

PassKey is open source; the code is public on GitHub.
