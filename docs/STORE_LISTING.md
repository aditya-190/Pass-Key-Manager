## Full description (4,000 characters max), 6.0.0
PassKey is an offline password manager that keeps your passwords, PINs and notes in an encrypted vault on your phone. No account, no cloud, no ads: your vault never leaves your phone.

WHY PASSKEY
• Offline by design: nothing you save is ever sent anywhere.
• Its own key, kept in Android's secure hardware and unlocked with your fingerprint or screen lock.
• A recovery password you choose, so a new fingerprint or screen lock never locks you out.

FILL PASSWORDS IN SECONDS
• Autofill in your apps and in Chrome, always after your fingerprint or PIN, with no entry names shown before that.
• On Android 14 and newer, PassKey can also fill passwords from the system sign-in sheet.
• Careful matching: look-alike apps and websites are not offered your logins.
• Save new logins straight from the sign-in screen.

TWO-FACTOR (2FA) CODES
• Add authenticator codes from the setup link or the key a website shows you.
• Tap a code to copy it. Copied codes and passwords are hidden from the clipboard preview and cleared automatically.

STRONG PASSWORDS AND PASSPHRASES
• A password generator with the length and characters you choose, or easy-to-type passphrases of 4 to 10 words.
• Safety Status finds weak and reused passwords.
• Password history keeps the last 10 versions of every password.

BACKUPS YOU CONTROL
• An encrypted backup file (.pkbak), protected by a password you choose, that you can restore on any phone.
• Verify a backup to check that it opens, without importing anything.
• A gentle reminder when your vault has no recent backup.

PRIVATE AND SIMPLE
• Auto-lock after a time you choose. Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked.
• Search by entry name or field name. Long-press the PassKey icon to add an entry or search.
• Light, dark, or the same as your phone. Delete with Undo.
• Crash reports and usage statistics are off until you choose to share them, and never include anything you save.

FOR EVERYONE
• 17 languages: English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, தமிழ், తెలుగు, عربي, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, Русский, 日本語, 한국어, 中文.
• Large-text support and screen-reader labels.
• Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

PassKey is open source; the code is public on GitHub.
